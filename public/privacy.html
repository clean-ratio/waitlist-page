Privacy

Clean Ratio

What we store

When you join the waitlist we store your name, your email address, the time you gave consent and the wording you agreed to. If you arrived from a campaign link we also keep its tags (for example, which newsletter sent you) and the page that referred you.

If you fill in the Lifestyle & Recovery Profile, we store your answers alongside your entry. Every question is optional.

Why we store it

To let you know when Clean Ratio launches, to send you occasional updates you agreed to, and to understand who the first rituals are for. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

Your consent

The consent box is required and never pre-ticked. You can withdraw consent at any time and we will stop emailing you.

Deleting your data

Write to us at tech@clean-ratio.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your entry and your profile together.

Who else sees it

Your details are kept in a database run for us by Supabase, and this site is hosted by Vercel. Neither uses your details for its own purposes. Nobody else receives them.
