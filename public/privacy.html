What we store

Your name and email address, the date you agreed to this use and the wording shown, and — if you answer the questions — your selections and answers about rest. Nothing else. We do not store payment details or sell personal data.

Why we store it

To associate your contact details with your answers and understand how people experience this site. Answers about rest and self-care are used only in aggregate. We do not use your details for marketing.

Your consent

The consent box is required and never pre-ticked. You can request deletion of your details and answers at any time.

Deleting your data

Write to us and ask. We will delete your row, and your answers with it, within seven days, and confirm when it is done. You can also ask for a copy of everything we hold about you.

Who else sees it

Our database host and our email provider, which sends a single receipt after submission. They do not use your data for their own purposes.
