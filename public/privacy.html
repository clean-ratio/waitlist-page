Privacy

Clean Ratio

What we store

The waitlist is not connected to a database yet. Your name, email, and answers are not sent to us or saved by this site. Form entries remain in your browser tab only and are cleared when you leave.

Why we store it

When a waitlist service is connected, we plan to use your email to send Clean Ratio updates. Until then, submitting the form does not add you to a list or send an email.

Your consent

The consent box is required and never pre-ticked. Because nothing is currently saved, there is no stored submission to delete.

Deleting your data

No waitlist entries or answers are currently retained by Clean Ratio.

Who else sees it

Your submission is not sent to a database or email provider.
